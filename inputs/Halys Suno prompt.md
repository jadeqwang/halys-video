Halys Suno prompt

\[Intro: solo harp, distant horn, a single low drone\]

\[Verse 1: hushed, almost whispered female voice, low drone and sparse piano\]  
The River Haylees, on the sixth year of the war  
Lydians and Medes, slew each other on the shore  
Sun burning on the bronze exchanging  
turns and strikes when light went strange.

\[Pre-Chorus: tremolo strings creep in, slow sub boom hits, choir hum\]  
A halo in the sky warriors odd  
by the vacant eye of a god

\[Chorus: full orchestra and choir arrive on a massive braam, slow pounding drums, voice opens up\]  
Daylight turned to shade  
eye of gods above  
Throw down your blade  
Go home to the ones you love

\[Break: everything cuts out, one held string note, a ticking pulse\]  
What was it like when reality suddenly broke

\[Build: pulsing synth ostinato, 16th-note strings, rising choir, riser\]  
Thaylees foretold the sun would go dark  
Warriors behold …  
\[Silence\]  
... a sudden spark

\[Final Chorus: key change up, full orchestra, massive choir, soaring soprano descant, giant drums\]  
Shadow turned to day  
Sunlight from above  
Throw down your blade  
Home to the ones you love

\[Climax: instrumental, relentless driving percussion and brass, choir chanting\]  
(throw down) (your blade)

\[Outro: three huge stop-time hits, silence, one stark minor chord, hard ending\]

\[End\]

Style: Epic hybrid orchestral trailer music with a haunting female vocal, like a slow cinematic trailer cover. Begins almost silent: solo harp, low drone, sparse piano, a hushed close-mic voice. Grows in waves: tremolo strings, choir, deep sub booms, massive brass braams, thunderous cinematic percussion, pulsing synth ostinato under the orchestra, risers and sudden silences. Ends with huge stop-time hits and a stark minor chord. Dark, mythic, awe-struck, sci-fi undertone. B minor, slow 72 BPM half-time feel.

Exclude styles: electric guitar, rock drums, pop, major key, trap hi-hats, fade out
